# Welcome to CleverAct AI

CleverAct AI is an **Agent-as-a-Service platform**. We give developers and businesses the infrastructure to embed intelligent AI agents directly into their products — so their end users can interact with their own data through natural conversation.

Train an agent on your knowledge base, connect it to your app's data via session variables, extend it with skills and tools, and deploy it in minutes. Your users get a smart assistant that knows their context, remembers their history, and can take actions on their behalf.

<Note>
  **Think of it this way:** Stripe is payments-as-a-service. Twilio is communications-as-a-service. CleverAct AI is **agents-as-a-service** — the API layer that makes any product AI-powered.
</Note>

## Why developers choose CleverAct AI

Most AI platforms give you a chatbot. CleverAct gives you the **backend infrastructure** to make your entire product intelligent:

- **Per-user isolation** — Each of your users gets their own agent session with their own context. Session variables (`user_id`, `plan`, `tenant_id`) flow into every AI interaction automatically.
- **Your data, their conversation** — Train agents on your docs, APIs, and databases. Your users ask questions in natural language and get answers grounded in real data.
- **Agents that act, not just talk** — Skills run sandboxed scripts, call your APIs, and execute logic. Pass credentials and variables per session so agents can operate on behalf of each user.
- **Agent memory** — Agents remember facts across sessions. Your users don't repeat themselves.
- **Any LLM, no lock-in** — OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Mistral, or any OpenRouter model. Switch per bot, no code changes.
- **Deploy in minutes** — One script tag for the widget, a few API calls for headless. No ML infrastructure to manage.

## How it works

```
Your Product (SaaS, App, Website)
    │
    │  Pass user context: { user_id, email, plan, tenant_id, ... }
    ↓
CleverAct AI API  ←──  Widget / Full-Page App / REST API / WebSocket
    │
    ├── Agent Runtime
    │   ├── LLM (OpenAI / Claude / Gemini / DeepSeek / ...)
    │   ├── Vector Search → Your Knowledge Base
    │   ├── Skills & Tools → Your APIs, scripts, MCP servers
    │   └── Memory → Persistent per-user facts & preferences
    │
    ├── Training Pipeline
    │   └── PDFs, DOCX, CSVs, Text, URLs → Auto-chunk → Embed → Index
    │
    ├── Analytics & Webhooks
    │   └── Conversations, usage, performance → Your systems
    │
    └── Live Chat & Handoff (optional)
        ├── AI ↔ Human Handoff (auto-detection)
        └── Team Routing (skill / workload / round-robin)
```

**The key idea:** Your users interact with the agent. The agent knows who they are (via session variables), what data to search (via your training data), and what actions to take (via skills). You control everything through the API and dashboard — the AI layer is invisible to your users.
